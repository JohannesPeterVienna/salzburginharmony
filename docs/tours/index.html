The walks

Eight walks through Salzburg

In the order we would suggest for a first visit. Each one is self-contained; the practical notes at the end say which walks join up. Times are generous estimates for a slow walk with stops, not schedules.

  • Walk 1 · The classic line · 8 stops · about 3–4 hours with the fortress

    From Mirabell Palace to the Fortress

    A self-guided Salzburg walk from Mirabell Palace through the gardens, over the Salzach and across the baroque squares up to Hohensalzburg Fortress, with Anton and Dora.

  • Walk 2 · Half a day out of town · 6 stops · about 4 hours including the way there and back

    Hellbrunn: the Water Palace in the South

    A self-guided visit to Hellbrunn Palace south of Salzburg: the old avenue, the trick fountains, the park and the hill, with dated opening times for 2026.

  • Walk 3 · Short, steep and still · 6 stops · about 1.5 hours

    Nonnberg: the Oldest Convent and the Quiet Hill

    A quiet self-guided walk from the Old Town up the Nonnbergstiege to Nonnberg Abbey, founded around 714, with the church, the view and the way down through Nonntal.

  • Leopoldskron Palace seen across the still water of the Leopoldskroner Weiher with Hohensalzburg Fortress on the hill behindWalk 4 · Flat, green, mostly quiet · 6 stops · about 1.5–2 hours

    Leopoldskron: a Palace Seen Across the Water

    A self-guided walk from Nonntal to the Leopoldskron pond, around the water with the palace and fortress in view, and out to the Moos meadows. The palace is private.

  • Wrought-iron guild signs hanging over the narrow Getreidegasse in Salzburg with tall house fronts on both sidesWalk 5 · Signs, passages and a bakery · 7 stops · about 2–2.5 hours

    Getreidegasse and the Lanes of the Old Town

    A self-guided walk through Salzburg’s Old Town: Alter Markt, the guild signs of the Getreidegasse, Mozart’s birthplace, the through-houses and St. Peter’s cemetery.

  • Walk 6 · Woods, walls and the best view of the Old Town · 7 stops · about 2–2.5 hours

    Kapuzinerberg: the Green Hill Across the River

    A self-guided walk up the Kapuzinerberg from the Linzergasse: the Capuchin monastery, the best view of the Old Town, the old bastions, the summit and the Steingasse.

  • Walk 7 · A day above the city · 6 stops · about 5 hours including travel

    Untersberg: Salzburg’s Mountain by Cable Car

    A self-guided day on the Untersberg: bus to Grödig, cable car to 1,776 m, the summit walk to the Salzburger Hochthron, legends and marble, with 2026 hours and closures.

  • Wooden stalls of the Salzburg Christmas market on Residenzplatz at dusk with strings of lights and the cathedral towers behindWalk 8 · Late November to early January · 7 stops · about 3 hours in town, half a day with Hellbrunn

    Winter Salzburg: Markets, Lights and Cold Clear Days

    A self-guided winter walk through Salzburg: the Christmas market on the cathedral squares (19 Nov 2026 – 1 Jan 2027), the ice rink, the fortress in Advent and what is closed.

Reading the practical boxes

Every walk starts with a “Before you go” box. The opening times, seasons, prices and closures in it come from the official pages of Salzburg Tourism (salzburg.info) or the operator, and each line names its source and the date we checked it, usually with the operator's own status date. Things change at short notice; if a line matters to your day, open the source link and look again on the morning you go. Where we could not verify a price, we do not print one.

Distances and durations are our own estimates for a slow walk with stops. Steps and gradients are described in words; none of the hill walks is step-free, and we say which parts of each route are.

Last checked: November 6, 2026.
