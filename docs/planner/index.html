Day planner

Plan your Salzburg day

Tick the stops you want from any of the walks, set how long you want at each, add a buffer, and the planner gives you a running schedule to copy, download or print. It lives in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

The planner adds up time at the stops only. Walking between stops, buses, queues, meals and opening hours are not calculated. Treat the total as the minimum your day needs, then look at the opening times in each walk's “Before you go” box.

Stops

  • Loading the planner needs JavaScript. Without it, open any walk and use your browser's print function.

How to use the planner well

Start with one walk, not three. A realistic Salzburg day holds one of the longer walks (Mirabell to the Fortress, Kapuzinerberg, Getreidegasse) plus one short one (Nonnberg, Leopoldskron) with a long lunch between them, or a single half-day outing to Hellbrunn or the Untersberg with a slow evening in town. The default minutes on each stop are generous, so a plan that adds up to five hours at the stops is a full day once walking and eating are included.

Order matters. The planner lets you move stops up and down; put the viewpoints in the middle of the day when the light is best, the markets and squares after dark in winter, and anything with a last admission early enough to make it. Museums and the fortress interiors are the stops most often cut short by closing times.

Set a start time and the planner shows a clock time for each stop, counting only the minutes before it. Add the walking time yourself: inside the Old Town most stops are five to ten minutes apart; the hills take fifteen to twenty minutes up; Hellbrunn and the Untersberg are half an hour or more from the centre by bus.

Where your plan is kept

Your selection, minutes, buffer and start time are stored in your browser's local storage under the key sih-plan, on your device only, until you clear it with the button above or delete this site's data in your browser. We cannot see it. If local storage is blocked, the plan works for the page you are on and is gone when you leave. “Copy as text” and “Download .txt” give you the plan as plain text to paste into your notes or send to a travel companion; “Print” uses your browser's print dialog, which can also save a PDF.
