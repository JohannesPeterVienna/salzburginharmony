The hosts

Alois and Theresa

Two invented Salzburgers who walk with you. They are not real people, not licensed guides and not photographs of anyone; they are AI-generated characters with a consistent face, a voice and a set of interests, created for this site so that the walks have a tone rather than a brochure.

Alois

An older man with a grey beard, a green felt hat with a tuft of feathers and a loden waistcoat. Alois talks about the prince-archbishops, the walls, the hills and the views. He prefers the footpath to the funicular, starts early, and has an opinion about every square. He is the one who tells you that fifty-five houses were cleared for the Residenzplatz, that nobody ever took the fortress, and that the Untersberg built the town.

Theresa

A woman with braided blond hair in a red bodice of the regional dress. Theresa knows the gardens, the markets, the bakery with the water wheel and what people in Salzburg do in each season. She tells you where to sit, what to eat, when the light is right and when to put the phone away. She leads the Hellbrunn and the winter walks.

How they were made

The portraits were generated with an image model from a written description and then cropped and prepared by us; they do not depict real persons. The dress they wear is the traditional clothing of the Salzburg region, as shown in the folklore museum at Hellbrunn, chosen because it is what visitors expect to see and what people here do wear at festivals. Their remarks on each walk are written by us with AI assistance and checked for facts against the sources named on each page; the opinions are deliberately theirs, not ours, and you are welcome to disagree with Alois about the funicular.

What they are not

They do not speak, animate or answer questions; there is no chat. They do not represent Salzburg Tourism, the City of Salzburg or any attraction. They are not based on any real person, historical figure or character from any book, film or play, and the walks do not retell any such story; they describe real places with their real history and leave the rest to you.
