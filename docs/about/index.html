About Salzburg in Harmony

An independent set of self-guided walks through Salzburg, Austria, made by AdStern, a small agency in Vienna, for visitors who want to understand the city rather than tick it off.

What the site offers

Eight walks, each with an introduction, a dated box of practical facts, a route sketch, six to eight stops with a text, a remark from one of two fictional hosts and an observation task, a walk mode that takes you through the stops one at a time, and a day planner that adds up your time at the stops. Everything is free to read and works without an account. Where a tour needs a ticket, we say so; we do not sell tickets, tours or anything on behalf of the places described.

Sources and checking

Opening times, seasons, prices and closures come from the official pages of Salzburg Tourism (salzburg.info), the operators' own sites (for example the Christmas market's site) and, for background history, Wikipedia and the published histories of the sites. Each walk lists its sources with links, and each practical line carries the date we checked it and, where the source gives one, the source's own status date. We checked all of them on 6 November 2026 and will re-check before each season. If we could not verify a figure, we do not print it. If you find an error, write to office@adstern.com; we correct quickly and note the date.

The descriptions of places are written in our own words from visits, the sources above and standard references. Observation tasks and the hosts' remarks are our own. The texts are produced with AI assistance and edited and checked by a person before publication; the hosts are AI-generated characters, which is stated wherever they appear.

Limits

This is not a map and not navigation. The route sketches are schematic; use a city map or the signposts. Durations are estimates for a slow walk. Nothing here is a guarantee of access, opening or safety; follow local notices, the rules of each site and your own judgement on the hills and the mountain. Private properties are described from outside only, and we ask you to respect that.

Photographs

Photographs on the walks are either our own, licensed stock images or clearly marked AI-generated illustrations; each image carries its credit in the caption or in the site's image list. Where an image is still missing, you see a placeholder with the description of the picture that belongs there. We do not use images from the attractions' websites without permission.

Advertising and money

The site shows advertising through Google AdSense on content pages, labelled “Advertisement”, never on the imprint, privacy, contact or error pages. We may offer a paid download pack of the walks for offline use in future; it will be clearly separate and the online walks will stay free. We have no commercial relationship with any place described, and no place has paid to be included or described favourably.
