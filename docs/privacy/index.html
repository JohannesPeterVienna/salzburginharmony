Data stored in your browser

Supplement of 24 November 2026. Walk mode stores the stop you reached in each walk (key sih-tour-…) and your chosen host (sih-host), and the day planner stores your plan (sih-plan), in your browser’s local storage on your device only. Your analytics decision is stored under tw-analytics-consent-v1 as described above. These entries are never sent to us or to third parties; they are needed to provide the functions you use (Section 165(3) TKG 2021). You can delete them with “Clear plan” on the planner page or by deleting this site’s data in your browser settings. If local storage is blocked, the website still works for the page you are on.
