Privacy policy

This policy explains which data is processed when you use www.salzburginharmony.com, for what purpose and on which legal basis, and what rights you have.

Controller

AdStern, Seilerstätte 8, 1010 Vienna, Austria, office@adstern.com (details in the imprint).

Hosting

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When a page is loaded, your browser transmits technical data – IP address, requested address, date and time, browser type, referring page – to GitHub's servers, which use it to deliver the page and to protect the service and may keep it in log files for a limited period. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a reliable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Walk progress, host choice and day plan

Walk mode stores which stop you reached in each walk (sih-tour-…), your chosen host (sih-host) and your day plan (sih-plan) in your browser's local storage, on your device only. These entries are never sent to us or to third parties; they are needed so that a walk and a plan survive a page change. Delete them with “Clear plan” on the planner page or by deleting this site's data in your browser settings. If local storage is blocked, the site still works for the page you are on.

Advertising with Google AdSense

This site shows advertising provided by Google AdSense, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and for users outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to display ads, limit how often an ad is shown, measure ad performance and prevent fraud.

The home page, the walks, the walk overview, the day planner, the hosts page and About can show advertising. The AdSense script, together with Google's consent message, is loaded on these pages; the positions are fixed boxes labelled “Advertisement”. The imprint, this privacy policy, the contact page, the shop page and the error page do not load the AdSense script and show no ads; Google's consent script is loaded there only if you explicitly click “Open Google's ad and U.S. privacy choices” in the statistics window.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, at www.youronlinechoices.eu. How Google uses information from sites that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform (IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) before cookies or similar technologies that need consent are used. Personalized ads require your consent. Non-personalized ads also use cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, reporting and fraud prevention, so they too require consent to storing and accessing information on your device. Without consent, Google can show only limited ads, which do not use cookies or local storage for personalised advertising. Before any Google script runs, Google Consent Mode is set to “denied” for all purposes. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the “Privacy settings” link in the footer of every page. Visitors from U.S. states with privacy laws can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the same link; a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser is respected.

Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to see which walks are read and used, so we can improve them. Before any Google script runs, Google Consent Mode is set to “denied” for advertising and analytics storage, and Google Analytics is switched off. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics is loaded only after you consent in the consent message to storing and accessing information on your device (TCF purpose 1), to Google as a vendor and to analytics. Elsewhere it is loaded only if the consent message reports analytics as granted or not requiring consent and has confirmed that you have not opted out under a U.S. state privacy law; missing or unclear signals keep it switched off. While Google's consent message is not displayed, Google Analytics is loaded only after you allow it in our statistics window (next section). If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics stays off. If you withdraw consent, Consent Mode returns to “denied” and Google Analytics is switched off immediately. Google signals and ad personalisation are disabled in our configuration. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. Event data is stored for the retention period set in our property and then deleted. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Transfers to Google LLC in the USA rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Statistics window

While Google's consent message is set up for this site but not displayed, we show our own small window (“Statistics only”) so that visits are counted only with your consent. It appears about three seconds after a page has loaded if Google's consent management has by then neither shown its message nor reported a decision, and has not reported that the GDPR does not apply to your visit. It asks only about statistics with Google Analytics. It is not consent to advertising and never changes the advertising settings of Google Consent Mode: ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation stay “denied”. “Allow” and “Decline” are equal choices. Closing the window with “×” or the Escape key, or making no choice, stores nothing and is not consent; Google Analytics then stays off and the window appears again on the next page.

If you allow it, Consent Mode sets only analytics storage to “granted” and Google Analytics 4 is loaded once, as described above; Google Analytics then sets cookies on your device (_ga, _ga_…), and data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. If you decline or later withdraw, Google Analytics is switched off at once, analytics storage returns to “denied” and the Google Analytics cookies set for this domain are deleted. Your choice is kept in your browser's local storage under the key sih-stats – a version number, “granted” or “denied” and the time of your choice – for 12 months; it is not sent to us or to Google. Storing this entry is strictly necessary to respect your decision. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via “Privacy settings” at the bottom of every page.

Google's consent management has priority: as soon as it shows its own message on a page, our window closes and does not appear again on that page. As soon as it reports a decision or that the GDPR does not apply to your visit, only that counts: a choice stored in our window is deleted and the Google Analytics rules above apply. If Google reports that you declined analytics, the Google Analytics cookies of this domain are deleted as well. The imprint, this policy, the contact page, the shop page and the error page load neither advertising nor Google Analytics; there “Privacy settings” opens the window only to view, change or withdraw the stored choice, and Google's consent script is loaded only on an explicit click.

Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns a contract or a request before a contract, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Messages are deleted when the matter is closed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

Shop (when available)

If a paid download is offered, the order is processed by Lemon Squeezy, LLC, as merchant of record on its own pages; what data it processes is described on the product page and in Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy before you order. This site itself does not process payment data.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at, dsb@dsb.gv.at.

No automated decisions, no profiling by us

We do not make automated decisions about you and do not build profiles. Advertising profiles, where you have consented, are built by Google and its partners as described above, not by us.
