Contact

Corrections, questions about a walk, image credits, press or advertising enquiries: write to us by email. We read everything and answer within a few working days.

Email: office@adstern.com
Post: AdStern, Seilerstätte 8, 1010 Vienna, Austria

Found a mistake?

Opening times and prices change at short notice. If a line in a “Before you go” box is wrong, tell us the walk, the stop and what you saw on site or on the operator's page, and we will correct it and note the date. The same goes for history: if you know a source that contradicts us, send it.

Places that would like to be mentioned

We do not take payment for inclusion or for favourable descriptions, and we do not publish advertorials in the walks. Advertising on the site is delivered by Google AdSense and labelled as such.

Using our texts and images

The texts are copyright AdStern. Short quotations with a link are welcome; reprinting whole walks is not. Images carry their own credits; the AI-generated host portraits may not be reused.

There is no contact form; a form would need a server and would collect data we do not want to hold. Email is enough, and what happens to it is in the privacy policy.
